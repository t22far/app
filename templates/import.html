Import from QuickBooks

Export your data from QuickBooks Online UK, then upload the CSV files below. Existing records are updated in place; adjustments are preserved.

How to export from QuickBooks UK

  1. Employees CSV
    Go to Payroll → Employees, click the export icon (top-right), choose Export to CSV.
  2. Time Off CSV
    Go to Payroll → Reports → Time Off Summary, set your date range, then click Export.

Expected columns: First name, Surname, Email address, Employment status, Job title, Department
